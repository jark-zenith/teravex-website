We believe intelligence should expand what people can do.

TERAVEX is an AI research company rooted in Kenya with a global outlook. We investigate emerging ideas, experiment with intelligent systems and turn promising research into practical technology.

Our work brings together curiosity, rigorous engineering and a belief that powerful technology should be useful to people everywhere.
